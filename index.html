Overview

About Me

I am a remote sensing and geospatial scientist currently pursuing my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering (Remote Sensing) at IIT Bombay.

My doctoral research focuses on developing a multi-sensor remote sensing framework for Sea Ice Thickness Estimation using Satellite Altimetry and Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR), under the guidance of Prof. RAAJ Ramsankaran and Dr. Vishnu Nandan.

Prior to joining IIT Bombay, I completed my M.Tech. at the Indian Institute of Remote Sensing (IIRS-ISRO), specializing in Satellite Image Analysis and Photogrammetry, where I developed deep learning frameworks for 3D semantic segmentation of point clouds for Historic Building Information Modelling (HBIM).

Focus Areas

Research Interests

Satellite Altimetry & SAR

Multi-sensor frameworks for polar sea ice thickness estimation, radar altimetry waveform analysis, and microwave remote sensing observations.

LiDAR & 3D Point Cloud Analysis

Terrestrial and airborne LiDAR scanning, dense 3D point cloud processing, and geometric digital twin modeling.

Deep Learning & Semantic Segmentation

Neural networks applied to 3D point clouds for architectural component classification, Nagara-style heritage datasets, and automated feature extraction.

Historic BIM (HBIM) & Photogrammetry

Close-range photogrammetry, archival cartography, 3D heritage documentation, and digital preservation of structural assets.

Geospatial AI & Environmental Monitoring

IoT- and GIS-driven environmental impact assessment, urban growth dynamics tracking over centuries, and landfill/dump yard hazard analysis.

Surveying & Spatial Analytics

Advanced GNSS/geodesy, spatial interpolation, route-choice risk modeling for waste management, and hydrological monitoring.
